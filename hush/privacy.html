Privacy Policy

feelit — by ORKLABS · Last updated: 16 November 2026

feelit ("the app", "we", "us") is a soundscape mixer for sleep, focus, meditation and relaxation, published by ORKLABS. We built it to respect your privacy. This policy explains exactly what data the app handles, why, and who processes it. It applies to the feelit iOS app.

What we do not collect

feelit does not require an account or login. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, location, or device identifiers. We do not use analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDKs of any kind, and we never sell or share your data for marketing.

What stays on your device

Your saved mixes, generated sounds, generated cover images, sleep recordings, timers and settings are stored locally on your device only. They are never uploaded to us and are permanently removed when you delete the app.

Smart Mix & AI generation

When you use Smart Mix or generate a sound or cover image, the app sends only the text you type (your scene description) and the goal you pick to our processing backend, which is operated by us on Cloudflare. That backend forwards your text to AI providers — Google (Gemini) to match sounds, and fal.ai / Stability AI to generate audio and cover images — purely to build your result and return it to you. Each provider processes this text under its own privacy policy; we send no account, name, email, or device identifier with it. This text is used only to fulfil your request. It is not linked to your identity, not used to build a profile of you, and not used for advertising. If you never use these features, no text ever leaves your device. All of the app's built-in sounds and presets play fully offline with no network use.

Sleep recordings

If you use Sleep Insights to record the night, audio is captured and analysed entirely on your device and stored only on your device. Recordings are never uploaded to us or anyone else, and are deleted when you remove a night or delete the app.

Purchases

feelit Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Payments are handled entirely by Apple — we never see your card, bank, or Apple Account details. To unlock Pro features across sessions and devices, the app sends Apple's signed purchase receipt to our backend solely to confirm the subscription is valid. The receipt contains no personal or payment information and is not stored.

Data retention

We do not maintain user accounts or a database of personal data. Smart Mix and generation requests are processed in transit to return your result and are not retained by us to identify you. Data held by Apple or by the AI providers is governed by their respective privacy policies.

Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data tied to your identity, there is nothing personal for us to export, correct, or delete on our side. You can delete all local data at any time by deleting the app. If you have questions about your data under the GDPR or other laws, contact us using the address below.

Children

feelit is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

For any privacy question or request, email info@orklabs.io. See also our Terms of Use.
