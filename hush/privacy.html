Privacy Policy

feelit — by ORKLABS · Last updated: 31 August 2026

feelit ("the app") is a soundscape mixer for sleep, focus, meditation and relaxation. We built it to respect your privacy. This policy explains exactly what data the app handles and why.

What we do not collect

feelit does not require an account. We do not collect your name, email, contacts, location, or device identifiers. We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind, and we never sell data.

What stays on your device

Your saved mixes, generated sounds, generated cover images, timers and settings are stored locally on your device only. They are never uploaded to us and are removed when you delete the app.

Smart Mix & AI generation

When you use Smart Mix or generate a sound or cover, the app sends only the text you type (your scene description) and the goal you pick to our processing backend, which forwards it to AI providers (Google Gemini for matching, and fal.ai / Stability AI for audio and image generation) to build your result. This text is processed to fulfil your request and is not linked to your identity, not used to build a profile, and not used for advertising. If you never use these features, no text ever leaves your device. All of the app's built-in sounds and presets play fully offline with no network use.

Purchases

feelit Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Payments are handled entirely by Apple — we never see your card or Apple account details. To unlock Pro features across sessions, the app sends Apple's signed purchase receipt to our backend solely to confirm your subscription is valid. The receipt contains no personal or payment information.

Children

feelit is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Data requests

Because we don't hold personal data tied to you, there's nothing personal to export or delete on our side. Deleting the app removes all local data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
